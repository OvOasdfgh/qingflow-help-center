## 关于其中que ID如何获取的问题

- 方式1、通过调用获取应用表单信息接口

[https://qingflow.com/help/docs/61dbf7eb328afe0041810d5c](https://qingflow.com/help/docs/61dbf7eb328afe0041810d5c)

![image.png](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2023/png/28884377/1690775227313-9dd0ffff-2b1c-4df6-81a5-4010fb148484.png)

- 方式2、通过后台开发者工具去获取，打开浏览器按Fn+12

点击network，然后清除数据

![image.png](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2023/png/28884377/1690775349546-3f2d3ccb-f88a-4b73-9cd8-b0f5a06b5b82.png)

点击打开某条数据，然后点击该条数据的applyid,在预览中找到到该字段的queid

![image.png](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2023/png/28884377/1690775455556-8b324192-cae6-4d50-8e4c-510f0f3a3af1.png)

- 方式3、直接发起一条数据，然后在右侧的apply，右击拷贝为curl，导入到postman中

![image.png](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2023/png/28884377/1690782397069-e42543a1-f063-43ba-b095-100cea0599a8.png)

![image.png](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2023/png/28884377/1690782466474-ea78a622-3818-4894-acae-911265027ef7.png)
